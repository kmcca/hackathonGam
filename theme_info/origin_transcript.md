## Context

This is the transcript of a video that explains the history of Hackathon. It uses the type of fairy-tale style language that I want the game to use. The Mark mentioned in this video is the Mark that will be the wizard in the game (his likeness is captured in the reference_images folder).

## Transcript

Long ago, in the bustling kingdom of Vericode, there lived a curious man named Mark.

Now, Mark was not a king, nor a wizard, nor a dragon slayer, yet he possessed a rare gift. He could see possibility where others saw only meetings, deadlines, and spreadsheets.

In those days, the people of Vericode worked diligently within the walls of their departments. Engineers engineered. Marketers marketed. Finance counted coins. Everyone knew their role, and few ventured beyond it.

But Mark dreamed of something more.

One autumn evening, while wandering through the halls after most had gone home, he imagined a great gathering where titles would be forgotten, ideas would run wild, and ordinary folk would build extraordinary things.

Many thought the notion peculiar.

"A whole day to experiment?" they asked. "To collaborate with strangers? To build something simply because it might be fun?"

But Mark merely smiled, and so he lit a spark.

The first hackathon was small, a humble affair filled with nervous excitement, hastily assembled teams, and more snacks than anyone truly needed.

Yet something remarkable happened.

Ideas leapt from minds like fireflies escaping a lantern. Problems that had lingered for months suddenly found solutions. Colleagues who had scarcely spoken became allies on grand adventures.

The spark became a flame.

Year after year, the gathering grew. Themes appeared, costumes emerged, tales were told, prizes were won. Laughter echoed through the kingdom's halls.

Hackathon became more than an event. It became a tradition.

Some years, the participants journeyed through enchanted forests. Some years they sailed pirate seas. Some years they wandered twisted fairy tales where heroes, villains, and curious creatures competed side by side.

Yet, no matter the theme, the true magic remained the same.

For a brief and wonderful time, everyone was invited to imagine, to create, to take a chance on an impossible idea.

And though countless people would add their own magic to the celebration over the years, the story always began the same way: with one man named Mark, a dream, a spark, and the simple belief that when clever people are given the freedom to play, remarkable things can happen.

And so the tale continues still, each new hackathon adding another chapter to the ever-growing book of Vericode legends, as all good fairy tales should.
