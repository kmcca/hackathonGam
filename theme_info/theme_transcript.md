## Context

This is the transcript of a video that introduces participants to this year's Hackathon theme, Page-Break. It uses the type of fairy-tale style language that I want the game to use. The wizard mentioned in this video is the Mark that will be the wizard in the game (his likeness is captured in the reference_images folder).

## Transcript

Once upon a time in a land called Burlington.

There were three Veracoders brainstorming for the next hackathon during their regular hospitable, hallowed huddle.

"Okay, back on track. We need to pick a theme early this year so we can start planning it."

From history to fiction, they studied stories for spark. They thought hard and sometimes hardly thought.

"Bananas. We haven't done them now, have we?"

"Are you serious?"

"What, like VeraFruit?"

"No, no. It's too close to VeraKitchen."

"Well, we did camp more than once."

"Guys, we're not doing fruit."

As with any huddle, sustenance was sought to stimulate their sparks.

"Speaking of bad ideas, I found this weird beer for you guys to try."

From his cold cooler of curiosities, he produced a glass bottle that gave a grotesque green glow.

"Where's that from?"

"Honestly, I don't remember. It was in the back of my garage fridge, same place you do your haunted house."

Full of curiosity and wonder, none hesitated to drink the nuclear nectar.

Shocked by the swill, they spewed the sip.

"That was worse than the fruit hackathon idea."

Boisterous banter and belching blew the beer onto the bound books. The gleaming grimoires glowed and gyrated grandly. The books burst open with a big boom, blasting them backward.

Fierce fiends and fairy fables flew from the folios, filling the room. Our friends were flummoxed, failing to find a fix.

As the pages perished and the pixies paraded, they pondered.

"What the heck was in this?"

But behold, another bound book burst into brilliance, bearing a brand new blow.

"Now you've done it."

Neither fiend nor fairy, a wondrous wizard walked in with wisdom and words.

"You've released all these fairy tale creatures into the real world. I expect you all are going to take care of that."

"Wait, what?"

"How?"

"Silence."

"Sorry, was that too loud?"

The mage did not mean to make a malicious mouthful, but he must manifest his message.

"Lucky for you and the path of your potent potable, these creatures may have been freed from their stories, but they are still bound to the essence of hackathon."

He highlighted the hosed Hackathon History Handbook.

"So do what Vera coders do best at your next hackathon, and you'll lure them back to their original stories. You can even split into teams to divide and conquer."

"You shall be known as Earth, Wind, and Fire."

Exalted and energized, they embraced their essence with their elected elements.

"Calm down. It's just team names, not powers."

Now our companions cringed comically in the Veracode Café.

"Well, my job's done. It's all on you now. I'm gonna play pinball."

Our companions stood courageous, committed to closing the chronicles.

"We got this."

Crucially, they could not conquer this crisis alone.

Will you answer the call for creativity and collaboration?

Introducing Hackathon 28. Page break.
